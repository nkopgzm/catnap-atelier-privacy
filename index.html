Catnap Atelier Privacy Policy

Operator: 知日見月工作室/郭智明 · Android · Updated November 22, 2026

Who operates the game

Catnap Atelier is developed and operated by the individual developer 郭智明 (Guo Zhiming), using the developer/studio name 知日見月工作室. YTTGames is a publishing brand name used for this game. In this policy, “we” refers to 郭智明 (知日見月工作室).

What the game saves

Core gameplay works locally on your device without an account. Puzzle progress, stamps, cat visits, bed decorations, music strips, and language and sound settings are kept in the app’s storage on your device.

Data, sharing, and voluntary rewarded ads

We do not upload saved games or gameplay activity to our own servers or require any login. When you choose to tap the optional “Watch Ad · Hint” button, the game requests a voluntary rewarded video ad through Google AdMob.

In compliance with the Google Play Families Policy for all-ages content, ads are strictly configured in child-directed, non-personalized mode (NPA) with G-rated ad content limits and the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) disabled. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process standard technical and diagnostic signals (such as IP-based country/region and ad interaction/crash diagnostics) solely to deliver and measure non-personalized ads.

Deleting your save

In Android settings, open Apps, choose Catnap Atelier, then Storage, and clear the app’s data. This deletes progress and settings from this device. We have no cloud copy to restore.

Support and privacy

For gameplay help, privacy questions or deletion of information you previously sent, email 郭智明 (知日見月工作室) at nkopgzm@gmail.com. On a phone, touch and hold the address to copy it.

If you email us, we receive your sender address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use these to respond to your request. Your game save is not attached automatically.

《猫咪午睡》隐私政策

运营主体：知日見月工作室/郭智明 · Android 版 · 更新于 2026 年 11 月 22 日

游戏运营主体

《猫咪午睡》由个人开发者郭智明开发和运营，开发者/工作室名称为“知日見月工作室”。“YTTGames”是本游戏使用的发行品牌名称。本政策中的“我们”指郭智明（知日見月工作室）。

游戏保存哪些内容

无需注册账号即可在本地游玩核心玩法。关卡进度、线章、猫咪来访记录、猫窝装饰、八音盒曲谱，以及语言和声音设置，都保存在你设备的应用存储中。

数据传输、共享与自愿激励广告

我们不要求注册登录，也不会将你的游戏存档上传至自有服务器。仅当你主动点击“看广告 · 提示”按钮时，游戏才会通过 Google AdMob 请求并播放一次自愿观看的激励视频广告，以解锁当前关卡的一步配平提示。

为严格遵守 Google Play《家庭政策》与全年龄适用要求，本应用中的 AdMob 广告已强制锁定为面向儿童的非个性化广告模式（NPA）、最高 G 级内容限制，并禁用 Android 广告 ID（AD_ID）。Google Mobile Ads SDK 仅在提供和统计非个性化广告的必要范围内处理基础设备与诊断信号。

如何删除存档

打开 Android 设置，进入“应用”，选择《猫咪午睡》，在“存储”中清除应用数据，即可删除这台设备上的进度和设置。我们没有可用于恢复存档的云端副本。

玩家支持与隐私联系

需要游戏帮助、咨询隐私问题，或希望删除你曾经发来的资料，可以联系郭智明（知日見月工作室）：nkopgzm@gmail.com。在手机上长按地址即可复制。

你主动发邮件时，我们会收到你的发件地址、正文和你选择添加的附件，用于回复并处理请求。邮件不会自动附上游戏存档。
