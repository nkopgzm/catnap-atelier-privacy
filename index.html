Catnap Atelier Privacy Policy

YTTGames · Android · Updated November 4, 2026

What the game saves

The game works offline without an account. Puzzle progress, stamps, cat visits, bed decorations, music strips, and language and sound settings are kept in the app’s storage on your device.

Data and sharing

The game does not upload saved games, device identifiers or gameplay activity to YTTGames or third parties. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics services.

Deleting your save

In Android settings, open Apps, choose Catnap Atelier, then Storage, and clear the app’s data. This deletes progress and settings from this device. YTTGames has no cloud copy to restore.

Support and privacy

For gameplay help, privacy questions or deletion of information you previously sent, email YTTGames at nkopgzm@gmail.com. On a phone, touch and hold the address to copy it.

If you email us, we receive your sender address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use these to respond to your request. Your game save is not attached automatically.

《猫咪午睡》隐私政策

YTTGames · Android 版 · 更新于 2026 年 11 月 4 日

游戏保存哪些内容

无需注册账号即可离线游玩。关卡进度、线章、猫咪来访记录、猫窝装饰、八音盒曲谱，以及语言和声音设置，都保存在你设备的应用存储中。

数据传输与共享

游戏不向 YTTGames 或第三方上传存档、设备标识符或游玩记录，也未接入广告或第三方分析服务。

如何删除存档

打开 Android 设置，进入“应用”，选择《猫咪午睡》，在“存储”中清除应用数据，即可删除这台设备上的进度和设置。YTTGames 没有可用于恢复存档的云端副本。

玩家支持与隐私联系

需要游戏帮助、咨询隐私问题，或希望删除你曾经发来的资料，可以联系 YTTGames：nkopgzm@gmail.com。在手机上长按地址即可复制。

你主动发邮件时，我们会收到你的发件地址、正文和你选择添加的附件，用于回复并处理请求。邮件不会自动附上游戏存档。
